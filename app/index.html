Chapter 05 // The Manifesto

Studio Philosophy

“My canvas is a visual ledger, transmuting personal isolation into societal critique.”

Started in 2011 with raw, gritty figurative oils in Pilani. Early pieces were unapologetic excavations of mental gravity, emotional density and spiritual hunger. Over 15 years of studio work, the canvas turned from an introspective mirror into an observation deck.

Blending old-master glazing with modern acrylics, clear bio-resin, 24k gold leaf and high-voltage pop iconography, recent works like the Richie series dissect how clout, luxury hype and identity are traded in a hyper-consumerist world.

Pilani, Rajasthan // Former studio: Sydney, NSW

01 // Impasto & Glazes
