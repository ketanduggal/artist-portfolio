The KD × PR Collection · First work

I

We made this in one night. Three, maybe four hours.

II

Every colour was mixed and measured before it touched the canvas.

III

We poured in layers. Even so, it kept surprising us.

IV

After it dried, we sealed it in resin, layer by layer, until it held the light like glass.

V

Underneath it all, one idea: balance.

“Two of us, one canvas, and a single night to get it right.”

Pilani, 2023

Ketan Duggal

Ketan paints slowly. Some of his canvases took two years. Pouring with Prateek is the opposite: one night, no second chances. He has worked in oil on canvas since 2011 and showed at Jawahar Kala Kendra, Jaipur, in 2012.

Prateek Roongta

Prateek trained as an architect, so he thinks in plans, lines and proportions. In a pour he mixes and measures every colour before a drop touches the canvas, and that's why the chaos holds together.
