Selected Works & Studio Archive (2011 — 2024)

Material Practice & Narrative

My practice is deeply anchored in the tactile reality of the medium. Through an evolving relationship with oils, bio-resins, and reclaimed surfaces, each canvas becomes an architectural space—a repository of memory and time.

The transition from the structured, figurative constraints of Pilani to the chaotic, densely layered surfaces of Delhi reflects an ongoing shedding of the unnecessary. Moving to Sydney introduced a luminous atmospheric quality, a search for an inner sanctuary manifested through sheer glazes and stark spatial voids.

In recent contemporary dialogues, the canvas breaks its two-dimensional confines, integrating pop iconography with archival materials to question hyper-commercialism. It is no longer just an image, but a physical object that invites a visceral, haptic engagement.
