From Hermit to Market: A Curatorial Note

The trajectory of Ketan Duggal's archive represents a profound psychological and geographic shift. It begins with the hermetic, structural tension of the Pilani Period, where dense oil glazes sought to map internal states of being—an intense period of introspection and foundational mastery.

The chaotic surfaces of Delhi broke these structures apart, allowing mixed media to introduce atmospheric turbulence, mimicking the overwhelming nature of the metropol. This was later distilled during the Sydney years into luminous resin landscapes and quiet studies of spatial balance.

Most recently, the Contemporary Pop series marks a radical externalization. Moving away from the purely emotional landscape, the work confronts hyper-commercialism, capitalism, and luxury branding directly, utilizing heavy mixed media panels as critique. The journey from the quiet introspection of 'The Urban Hermit' (2012) to the biting satire of 'Richie Carrying Gifts' (2024) encapsulates an artist moving from the deep internal to the harsh external world.
