CHAPTER 02 // STUDIO DISCIPLINES

The 4 Studio Disciplines

Old-master layering meets hyper-modern fluid chemistry. How raw ideas are materialized in the Pilani studio.

01 // IMPASTO & GLAZES

Heavy Oil Glazing

Traditional multi-layered oil glazes built over months of studio retreat. Creating rich, light-trapping shadows and emotional density.

02 // LIQUID FLOW STATES

Clear Bio-Resin

High-gloss, ultra-transparent spatial surfaces that freeze fluid acrylic pigments in suspended glass-like animation.

03 // SANCTUARY ILLUMINATION

24K Gold Guilding

Hand-applied genuine 24-karat gold leaf backgrounds, offering an ethereal sanctuary glow against dark expressive silhouettes.

04 // HYPE CULTURE CRITIQUE

Pop Luxury Satire

Maximalist street collage examining modern flex culture, luxury brand obsessions, and how status is traded in contemporary society.

CHAPTER 05 // THE MANIFESTO

Studio Philosophy

"My canvas is a visual ledger — transmuting personal isolation into societal critique."

Started in 2011 with raw, gritty figurative oils in Pilani. Early pieces were unapologetic excavations of mental gravity, emotional density, and spiritual hunger. Over 13 years of studio grind, the canvas transformed from an introspective mirror into an observation deck.

Blending old-master glazing with modern acrylics, clear bio-resin, 24k gold leaf, and high-voltage pop iconography, recent works like the Richie series dissect how clout, luxury hype, and identity are traded in a hyper-consumerist world.
