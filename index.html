Studio Philosophy

My practice is rooted in the tactile exploration of oils, glazes, and mixed media. It is an ongoing dialogue between structure and dissolution, where each layer acts as a record of time and memory.

The transition from the structured forms of Pilani to the chaotic layers of Delhi, and eventually the atmospheric light of Sydney, represents a continuous shedding of the unnecessary. What remains is a distillation of emotion, captured through the physicality of the medium itself.

In recent works, the canvas ceases to be a mere surface and becomes an object in its own right, breaking the two-dimensional plane to invite a visceral, haptic engagement.
