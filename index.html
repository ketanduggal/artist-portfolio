Artist Statement
"My practice is a visual record of transmuting personal isolation into societal critique."

Beginning in 2011 with raw, figurative oil works created in Pilani, my early paintings functioned as visceral excavations of human psychology, emotional density, and spiritual longing. Over a decade of active studio practice, my canvas evolved from an internal mirror into an external observation deck.

Merging classical oil glazing techniques with modern acrylics, fluid resin, gold leaf, and high-contrast pop iconography, recent works—including the Richie series—investigate how value, identity, and status are negotiated in a brand-obsessed world.

Based in Pilani, Rajasthan • Former Studio: Sydney, Australia
