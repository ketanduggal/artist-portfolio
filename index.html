About the Artist

Bio & Artistic Journey

Ketan Duggal's artistic journey began in the quiet town of Pilani, Rajasthan, where he first discovered the transformative power of oil paint while balancing life as a national athlete and guitarist. This dual identity—the disciplined physicality of sport and the expressive freedom of music—would later inform his approach to canvas, creating works that possess both rhythmic movement and emotional depth.

The Pilani Period (2011–2014) represents his foundational years, characterized by hermetic psychological oils and heavy structural tension. Working from Birla School and his home studio, Duggal developed a distinctive visual language that mapped internal states of being through dense glazes and introspective figurative studies. Works from this period, such as "The Urban Hermit" and "Complex Agony," reveal an artist deeply engaged with questions of solitude and identity.

Transitioning to Delhi (2015–2017), Duggal's practice underwent a dramatic evolution. The chaotic surfaces of the metropolis broke his earlier structures apart, allowing mixed media to introduce atmospheric turbulence. This period saw the emergence of more kinetic brushstrokes and textural experimentation, as seen in "Blue Winds" and "Strangers in the night," works that capture the overwhelming nature of urban existence.

The Sydney years (2020–2021) brought a radical distillation of these influences. Luminous resin landscapes and quiet studies of spatial balance replaced the turbulent surfaces of Delhi. Works like "Study of Balance" and "Querencia" reflect an artist in search of inner sanctuary—a quiet place of strength and focus amidst geographic transience. The introduction of bio-resin over acrylic planes created glossy depths that spoke to new conceptual territories.

Most recently, Duggal's Contemporary Pop series (2022–2024) marks a radical externalization. Moving away from purely emotional landscapes, the work now confronts hyper-commercialism, capitalism, and luxury branding directly. Heavy mixed media panels serve as both surface and critique, with pieces like "Richie Make Money" and "Richie Carrying Gifts $ High Fashion Brands" utilizing pop imagery and satirical commentary to examine consumer culture.

This evolution—from the quiet introspection of early Pilani works to the biting satire of contemporary pop-art—encapsulates an artist moving from the deep internal to the harsh external world, always maintaining a commitment to material exploration and authentic expression.

Exhibition Timeline

Chronological Archive & Milestones

2024

Contemporary Pop Series Completion

Completion of the Richie series, marking a radical shift toward pop-satire and consumer culture critique.

2021

Sydney Residency Conclusion

Final works from the Sydney period, including "Querencia," completed before return to India.

2020

Resin Landscape Exploration

Introduction of bio-resin techniques leading to the "Study of Balance" series and spatial investigations.

2017

Delhi Period Conclusion

Completion of mixed media works including "Elucidated," marking the end of metropolitan exploration.

2015

Transition to Delhi

Relocation to Delhi marked by dramatic shift in palette and approach toward urban atmospheric painting.

2014

Pilani Period Mastery

Completion of foundational psychological oils including "The Doomed One" and "The One Who Knew."

2011

Artistic Practice Begins

First major work "Complex Agony" created at Birla School, Pilani, establishing foundational oil techniques.
