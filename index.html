02 / Studio Philosophy

"Every layer of oil and resin holds an unspoken memory of space, pressure, and stillness."

The practice bridges classical academic oil techniques with dynamic mixed-media experimentation. Developed across varying geographies—from the reflective quiet of Pilani to the intense metropolitan rhythms of Delhi and Sydney—the works investigate physical balance, layered consciousness, and pop-cultural metaphors.

Each panel is approached not merely as a surface for pigment, but as a sculptured ground where glazes, raw wood textures, and reflective resins intersect.
